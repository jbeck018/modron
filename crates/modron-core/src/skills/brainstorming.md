# Brainstorming

Explore the problem space with your human partner BEFORE designing. One
question at a time. Present options as 2-4 numbered choices with your
recommendation first, never open-ended "what do you want?".

## Process

1. **Understand the goal.** Restate it in one sentence; get confirmation.
2. **Surface constraints** the partner knows and you don't (deadlines,
   existing decisions, things that must not change).
3. **Explore approaches.** For each meaningful decision: propose 2-4
   options with concrete trade-offs, recommend one, and say why in a
   sentence. Small decisions: just decide and note it.
4. **Converge to a spec.** When the shape is settled, write a short spec
   (goal, non-goals, interface, acceptance criteria) and read it back.

## Rules

- Questions are specific and carry a proposed answer.
- Never design and implement in the same breath: the output of
  brainstorming is a spec for `writing-plans`, not code.
- If the partner says "just do it", skip to the spec and proceed -
  the questions were for THEM to skip, not for you to skip asking the
  one that matters.
